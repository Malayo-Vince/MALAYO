Introduction

The Philippines is home to some of the most stunning natural wonders in Southeast Asia. From quiet beaches to towering volcanoes and historic ruins, here are four spots every traveler should see.

Dancalan Beach, Bulusan, Sorsogon

Dancalan Beach is a quiet shoreline in the town of Bulusan in Sorsogon, at the southern tip of the Bicol Region. Its calm, laid-back setting makes it a good place for swimming, picnics, and relaxing away from the crowds.

Bulusan Volcano Park, Sorsogon

Bulusan Volcano Natural Park protects one of the most active volcanoes in the Philippines, along with the surrounding forest and Bulusan Lake. It is a favorite for nature walks, bird watching, and enjoying fresh mountain air.

Mayon Volcano

Located in Albay, Mayon is famous for its symmetrical cone shape, considered the most perfectly formed volcano in the world, and is a popular backdrop for photography and hiking.

Cagsawa Ruins, Albay

Cagsawa Ruins in Daraga, Albay, are the remains of a Franciscan church buried when Mayon erupted in 1814. Only the bell tower still stands, and it has become one of the most photographed landmarks in Bicol, framed by the volcano behind it.
